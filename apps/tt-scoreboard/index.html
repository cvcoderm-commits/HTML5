📷 Camera auto scoring (beta)

Point the camera at the table from the side so the net runs top to bottom in the picture, then drag the net slider onto the net. When the ball stops moving, the player on the side where it was last seen loses the rally. Each call waits 3 seconds so you can cancel it.

Tap a score to add a point. Keys: A/← left point, L/→ right point, Z undo, S swap ends, F fullscreen.
